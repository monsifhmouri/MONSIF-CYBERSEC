## About

MONSIF CYBERSEC is a cybersecurity brand focused on server security monitoring, infrastructure protection, threat detection and security operations.

Our work is built around visibility, proactive monitoring, careful analysis and reliable infrastructure practices. We help organizations understand what is happening inside their systems, spot suspicious activity early, and respond in a structured, well-documented way.

## Mission

To help businesses understand, monitor and improve the security of their digital infrastructure through continuous monitoring, security analysis, threat detection and professional incident response.

## Vision

To build a trusted cybersecurity brand focused on protecting digital infrastructure, improving security awareness and helping organizations run reliable, secure systems.

## Team Structure

The company is being built role by role. Below is the full picture of the people we need, grouped by function.

### Leadership

**Founder and Security Lead** (filled)
Sets technical direction, defines service standards, reviews incident reports and leads client communication in the early stage.

### Security Operations

**SOC Analyst (Tier 1 / Tier 2)**
- Monitor alerts and security events across client environments
- Triage, escalate and document findings
- Maintain playbooks and shift handover notes
- *Looking for:* solid networking and Linux fundamentals, familiarity with log analysis, clear written English or French

**Threat Detection Engineer**
- Write and tune detection rules and correlation logic
- Reduce false positives and improve alert quality
- Map detections to known attacker techniques
- *Looking for:* experience with SIEM or log pipelines, understanding of attacker behavior, scripting skills

**Incident Response and Forensics Analyst**
- Lead investigations from first alert to final report
- Collect and preserve evidence, build timelines
- Recommend containment and recovery actions
- *Looking for:* experience with Linux forensics and log review, calm and methodical working style

### Infrastructure

**Linux Security Engineer**
- Perform security configuration reviews and hardening assessments
- Review SSH, firewall, service and permission setups
- Produce clear remediation guidance
- *Looking for:* strong Linux administration background, familiarity with common hardening benchmarks

**Monitoring and Reliability Engineer**
- Operate availability and uptime monitoring for websites and servers
- Build dashboards and alerting thresholds
- Support capacity and performance visibility
- *Looking for:* experience with monitoring stacks, networking, and basic automation

### Engineering

**Security Automation Developer (Go / Python)**
- Build internal tooling for data collection, alert enrichment and reporting
- Maintain the company's open-source and internal utilities
- *Looking for:* Go or Python proficiency, clean code habits, security-minded development

**Web and Platform Developer**
- Maintain the company website and client-facing portals
- Handle deployment, performance and secure configuration
- *Looking for:* semantic HTML/CSS, JavaScript, accessibility and security basics

### Business and Communication

**Technical Writer and Reporting Specialist**
- Turn technical findings into clear, structured client reports
- Maintain documentation, templates and knowledge base
- *Looking for:* strong writing in English and French, ability to explain technical issues plainly

**Client Relations and Business Development**
- First point of contact for inquiries and onboarding
- Translate client needs into service scopes
- *Looking for:* communication skills, understanding of the cybersecurity market, ethical approach to sales

**Operations and Administration**
- Handle contracts, scheduling, invoicing and internal coordination

## Working Principles

- **Visibility first.** You cannot defend what you cannot see.
- **Evidence over assumptions.** Every conclusion should be backed by data.
- **Clear reporting.** Findings are written so that both engineers and decision makers can act on them.
- **Responsible practice.** We work only within authorized scope and follow ethical, legal guidelines.
- **No overpromising.** No service can guarantee complete protection. We commit to rigor, honesty and continuous improvement.

## Who We Want to Work With
